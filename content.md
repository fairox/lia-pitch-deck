# *THE LIA PROFESSIONAL & BUSINESS PARK*
### Unlocking Long-Term Institutional Value & Financial Resilience
*A Strategic Proposal by Design X Threshold JV* *Date:* May 2026
*Presented to:* The CEO, Management, and Council of the Lesotho Institute of Accountants (LIA)
## *SLIDE 1: THE EXECUTIVE SUMMARY*
### *The Core Thesis: Moving From a Building to an Ecosystem*
 * *The Mandate:* LIA’s statutory mission is to develop, enhance, and regulate the accounting profession in Lesotho. To do this sustainably, the Institute must reduce reliance on government subvention and membership fees alone.
 * *The Pitfall:* Approaching Plot 12284-10006 at 5 Orpen Road simply as an office block with retail space presents immense financial and operational risks.
 * *The Vision:* We propose transforming the site into the *LIA Professional & Business Park*. This will serve as a 7-day-a-week professional ecosystem where LIA can train, examine, convene, and generate diversified, recurring income.
 * *Strategic Recommendation:* Execute a rigorous *Pre-Feasibility and Concept Phase* before committing capital or selecting rigid delivery models.
## *SLIDE 2: THE CURRENT CONTEXT & STRATEGIC ALIGNMENT*
### *Direct Response to LIA’s 2024–2029 Strategic Plan*
 * *Strategic Targets:* LIA’s current directives specifically mandate implementing defined revenue models, optimizing resource utilization, and establishing income-generating initiatives through rentable space.
 * *The Spatial Solution:* * *LIA House (Institutional Anchor):* Council rooms, compliance support, and a high-end professional member lounge.
   * *100-Station Digital Exam & Training Halls:* Flexible spaces that serve LIA examinations by day and external corporate or block-release training by night/weekends.
   * *Commercial Engine:* High-tier rental offices for audit/legal firms, a conference center, and highly curated retail (café, business center).
## *SLIDE 3: SITE ANALYSIS & PHYSICAL CONSTRAINTS*
### *Plot 12284-10006, Old Europa, Maseru*
 * *Site Area:* Approximately 1,395 square meters.
 * *The Urban Reality:* Located on Orpen Road, this prime location commands premium commercial rentals but demands strict urban planning integration, parking efficiencies, and traffic management.
 * *Development Assumptions:*
   * Allowable GBA: ~2,500 sqm via a multi-story layout.
   * Indicative Baseline Cost: *M45,000,000* (Total Development Cost benchmarked for regional high-tier mixed-use assets).
 * *Design Metric:* Every square meter must be designed for flexibility. Meeting rooms must convert to CPD venues; exhibition spaces must scale effortlessly.
